# Heroes — Context Transfer

## Vision

Heroes is an exploration of computational portraiture where live musical performance reveals, transforms, and converses with portraits through semantic mappings rather than direct parameter mappings.

The project also serves as a laboratory for developing SCHEMA.md, a language for describing computational art independent of any one platform.
